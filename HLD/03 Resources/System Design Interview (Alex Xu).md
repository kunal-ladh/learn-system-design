# System Design Interview (Alex Xu)

> [!abstract] Why this resource
> Interviewers at most product companies have read this book, so its four-step format (requirements → high-level design → deep dive → wrap-up) is the format they expect to see. Use it to calibrate the *shape* of a 45-minute answer; the decision sheets in [[Problem Bank MOC]] exist so you do not reproduce its diagrams from memory.

## When to read it

One chapter per problem in the week you attempt that problem, after your own design is written. The ByteByteGo site hosts the paid course version; the printed volumes 1 and 2 are the same material.

## What to look for

*Pre-filled from the resource itself so you know what you are looking for. It is not a substitute for reading: the note stays `unread` until you have.*

- The site describes text-based courses with step-by-step illustrations; preview chapters and the curriculum can be viewed before buying.
- The System Design Interview course is one of several (coding patterns, OO design, ML and mobile system design are separate).
- The chapters you want are the rate limiter, consistent hashing, key-value store, unique ID generator, URL shortener, notification system, news feed, chat and search autocomplete.
- Every chapter runs the same four-step script with a capacity estimate up front — copy the script, not the numbers.
