## What to look for

*Pre-filled from the resource itself so you know what you are looking for. It is not a substitute for reading: the note stays `unread` until you have.*

- The talk is based on the first edition of his book and a Stanford software design course.
- His background spans 14 years in industry and 14 as a Berkeley professor; systems include Sprite, Tcl/Tk, log-structured file systems, Raft and RAMCloud.
- Expect the core ideas of deep vs. shallow modules, information hiding and 'complexity is anything that makes software hard to understand or modify'.
- The video description promises how to — and how not to — design software.
