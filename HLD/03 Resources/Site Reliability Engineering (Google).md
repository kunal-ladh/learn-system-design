> [!abstract] Why this resource
> The Observability & Reliability category is largely a condensation of this book. When an interviewer asks 'how would you know this is working?' or 'what happens under overload?', chapters 4, 6, 21 and 22 are the answers.

## When to read it

Chapter 4 (SLOs) and chapter 6 (Monitoring) in week 11 of [[Start Here]]; chapters 21–22 (Handling Overload, Addressing Cascading Failures) before any problem with flash traffic; chapter 15 (Postmortem Culture) when you write up a work incident.

## What to look for

*Pre-filled from the resource itself so you know what you are looking for. It is not a substitute for reading: the note stays `unread` until you have.*

- Chapter 4 is Service Level Objectives and chapter 6 is Monitoring Distributed Systems.
- Chapters 14–15 cover Managing Incidents and Postmortem Culture: Learning from Failure.
- Chapters 21–22 are Handling Overload and Addressing Cascading Failures — the source for load shedding and retry-storm thinking.
- Chapter 23 is Managing Critical State: Distributed Consensus for Reliability.
- Appendix B is a collection of best practices for production services; the whole book is CC BY-NC-ND, free online.
