# Building Microservices

> [!abstract] Why this resource
> It is the counterweight to microservices hype: Newman spends as much time on when not to split as on how. Chapters 4, 6, 9 and 13 map directly onto the notes on sync vs. async communication, sagas, testing strategies and scaling.

## When to read it

Chapter 1 (free via the O'Reilly link on the page) and chapter 6 (Workflow) after the Saga and Distributed Transactions notes; chapter 13 (Scaling) with [[Horizontal vs Vertical Scaling]].

## What to look for

*Pre-filled from the resource itself so you know what you are looking for. It is not a substitute for reading: the note stays `unread` until you have.*

- Chapter 4 covers asynchronous vs. synchronous calls and request-response vs. event-driven collaboration styles.
- Chapter 6 (Workflow) compares sagas and distributed transactions for business processes that span services.
- Chapter 9 (Testing) covers the problems of end-to-end tests and how consumer-driven contracts and in-production testing help.
- Chapter 10 covers the shift from static monitoring to observability.
- Chapter 13 (Scaling) lays out the 'four axes of scaling' and how they combine.
