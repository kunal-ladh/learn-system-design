## What to look for

*Pre-filled from the resource itself so you know what you are looking for. It is not a substitute for reading: the note stays `unread` until you have.*

- The debt metaphor is useful for communication, so the question is how to deal with the debt, not whether it is debt.
- The useful distinction is between prudent and reckless debt.
- Deliberate prudent debt: ship now with a known shortcut that may never be worth paying down.
- Reckless debt includes quick-and-dirty code from a team that knows better.
- Prudent inadvertent debt — realising the right design a year in — is inevitable and should be expected.
