## When to read it

Lab 3 (Raft) after reading [[Raft Paper]], only once the sprint is done; the lecture notes and papers are useful as a reading list before that.

## What to look for

*Pre-filled from the resource itself so you know what you are looking for. It is not a substitute for reading: the note stays `unread` until you have.*

- A 12-unit graduate subject with lectures, readings, programming labs, an optional project and exams.
- Major topics: fault tolerance, replication and consistency.
- Five labs: MapReduce, a key-value server, Raft, a fault-tolerant key-value service on Raft, and sharding.
- Much of the class is case studies of real distributed systems.
- Archived course sites from 2000 to 2025 are linked.
