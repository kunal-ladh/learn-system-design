## What to look for

*Pre-filled from the resource itself so you know what you are looking for. It is not a substitute for reading: the note stays `unread` until you have.*

- Simple != easy; to complect is to interleave, entwine, braid.
- Choosing easiness moves fast but accumulated complexity kills the project over time.
- Benefits of simplicity: ease of understanding, change, debugging; flexibility.
- Constructs called complex include state, objects, inheritance, conditionals; simpler ones include values, functions, data, queues and declarative data manipulation.
- Intertwining raises complexity combinatorially; avoid connecting objects directly — use queues.
