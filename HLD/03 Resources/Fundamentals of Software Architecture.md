# Fundamentals of Software Architecture

> [!abstract] Why this resource
> Most of the Product Engineering notes ask you to choose between styles for reasons you can state. This book supplies the vocabulary (architecture characteristics, trade-off analysis, fitness functions) and the architecture katas you can practise with. It matters more for SDE-3 conversations than for SDE-2 rounds, so it is low priority until the sprint is over.

## When to read it

The architecture styles catalogue and the chapters on architecture characteristics after week 12 of [[Start Here]]; the katas on the site are good group practice.

## What to look for

*Pre-filled from the resource itself so you know what you are looking for. It is not a substitute for reading: the note stays `unread` until you have.*

- The book examines architecture patterns, component identification, coupling, cohesion, partitioning and granularity.
- It includes a catalogue of architecture styles with comparisons, and the notion of architecture characteristics.
- Architecture Katas are described as a small-group (3–5 people) exercise for practising what an architect does.
- It also covers the soft side: team management, meetings, negotiation and presentations.
- Its aim is to add rigour — repeatable results, metrics and concrete valuations — to architecture decisions.
