# Weekly Reviews

> [!abstract] The routine
> Every Sunday (or whichever day you choose), create a note here from `Templates/Weekly Review Template.md` (Obsidian: *Insert template*). Set `period_start` (Monday) and `period_end` (today) in its properties. The automatic sections list the concepts you reviewed, the problems you worked and the projects you touched in that period, so the only things you write by hand are the ones that matter: what you can now explain, what surprised you, what is unclear, and three priorities for next week.

**Map:** [[Engineering Dashboard]] · [[Start Here]]

## What a good review contains

- **What I learned** — notes that moved from `not-started` to `reading`/`understood`.
- **What I can now explain independently** — the honest list; these are the notes whose `confidence` becomes 3.
- **What I designed or implemented** — problems and project milestones.
- **What surprised me / what remains unclear** — this is the raw material for the dashboard's knowledge-gap tags (`gap/explain`, `gap/decision`, `gap/internals`, `gap/hands-on`).
- **Which assumptions I need to validate** — turn each into a practical exercise in a concept note or a technical challenge in a project.
- **Three priorities** — only three; they become next week's *Today's three moves* on the dashboard.

## Progress measures worth tracking (not vanity)

Concepts at confidence ≥ 3 · design problems `reviewed` · projects that reached M2 (measured) · architecture decisions documented · incidents or performance problems you investigated at work and wrote up. Counts of notes created or pages read are not progress.
