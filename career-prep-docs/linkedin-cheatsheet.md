```

about recommendations. Would you be open to writing a short one?

If it helps, you could mention:
- [specific story 1]
- [specific story 2]
- [specific story 3]

Happy to return the favor.

Thanks!
Kunal
```

---

## Do NOT

- Put "looking for a job" in your headline
- Add a profile photo banner with "OPEN TO WORK" badge (preserves dignity in negotiations)
- Endorse skills you don't actually have
- Post motivational quotes / generic content
- Comment "great post!" — adds nothing, hurts algo
