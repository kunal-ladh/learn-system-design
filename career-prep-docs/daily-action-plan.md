## The 4 Daily Non-Negotiables

These happen **every day, no exceptions**. They're the spine.

- [ ] **1 problem minimum** (DSA) — even on bad days, even if you peek at editorial
- [ ] **Mistakes log update** — 1 line minimum
- [ ] **Walk** — 20 min at night for health (you said you're prioritizing this)
- [ ] **8h sleep window** — you're up at 5:00, so lights out by 9:30 PM ideally

If you do nothing else and just hit these 4, the streak stays alive.

## The 3-Bullet Daily Journal (End of Day, 60 seconds)

At lights-out, write 3 bullets in a journal/notes app:

1. **Did I hit the 4 non-negotiables today?** (yes/no for each)
2. **What was the one thing I learned today that I want to remember?**
3. **What's the FIRST thing I'll do tomorrow at 5:00 AM?**

That third bullet matters most. Pre-deciding kills morning procrastination.

## Phase-Specific Adjustments

### Weeks 1-2: Lock-Down Phase
- DSA focus is **rebuilding lukewarm topics** — don't worry about new topics
- LLD is small problems (Logging, Rate Limiter, Vending Machine)
- HLD is **reading-heavy** (Grokking PDFs) — only 1 actual HLD problem in Week 2
- Applications: NOT YET. Focus on prep.

### Weeks 3-4: Build Phase
- DSA = your scariest topic (Graphs) — push through it
- LLD = your most-asked problems (Parking Lot, ATM)
- HLD = framework drilling on easy problems
- **APPLICATIONS START END OF WEEK 4** to Tier C companies

### Weeks 5-6: Stretch Phase
- DSA = your other scary topic (DP)
- LLD = concurrency-heavy problems (LRU, BookMyShow)
- HLD = medium problems (Instagram, WhatsApp)
- Applications: scale to 20-25/week, add Tier B

### Weeks 7-8: Interview Mode
- DSA = mocks + weak-area drilling
- LLD = advanced (Text Editor, Elevator)
- HLD = hard problems + 3 mocks
- Active interview pipeline — adjust schedule around interview times
